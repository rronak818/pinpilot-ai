Privacy Policy

PinPilot AI — Shopee Deals & Steals

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Shopee Deals & Steals handles information in connection with PinPilot AI, a privately operated research and marketing platform being developed for its own Pinterest affiliate-marketing business.

PinPilot AI is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest, Inc.

2. Information and data

Depending on implemented features, enabled integrations, and granted permissions, PinPilot AI may process:

  • Research queries, niche information, and product research.
  • Affiliate product and program information.
  • Pinterest account information made available through authorized Pinterest API permissions.
  • Pin, Board, and account analytics data where authorized.
  • Content drafts and information needed to manage approved publishing workflows.
  • Technical logs necessary to operate and troubleshoot the application.

The specific information processed depends on the features actually implemented and the permissions granted.

3. How information is used

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Researching affiliate-marketing niches and products.
  • Analyzing Pinterest trends and permitted account metrics.
  • Organizing affiliate product information.
  • Supporting content creation and management.
  • Maintaining application security and reliability.

PinPilot AI is intended for the owner's own business use. It is not currently offered as a public service for managing other people's Pinterest accounts.

4. Pinterest data and permissions

Pinterest information will only be accessed through authorized Pinterest integrations and the permissions granted by the authenticated account.

PinPilot AI will use Pinterest data only for permitted purposes and in accordance with applicable Pinterest Developer Terms and policies.

Pinterest data will not be sold or shared with unrelated third parties. Storage, retention, and deletion of Pinterest data will follow Pinterest's applicable requirements and any required permissions or approvals.

Access to Pins and Boards belonging to other Pinterest users is not intended.

5. Storage and security

PinPilot AI is designed as a local-first application. Research and application information may be stored in local databases or files as necessary for implemented features.

Reasonable technical measures will be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and deletion

Information will be retained only as needed for its intended business or operational purpose, subject to applicable legal and platform requirements.

Information will be deleted when it is no longer required, where deletion is permitted and required by applicable policies and obligations.

7. Third-party services

PinPilot AI may interact with third-party services such as Pinterest and affiliate networks. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Users should review the applicable policies of those services when using their platforms.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PinPilot AI develops, integrations change, or applicable requirements evolve. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
