About PinPilot AI

PinPilot AI is a privately operated research and marketing automation platform being developed for its owner's Pinterest affiliate-marketing business.

Its intended capabilities include niche and product research, Pinterest trend analysis, affiliate product organization, content creation support, and Pinterest account performance analysis.

Human-supervised publishing

The platform is designed to keep publishing and other account-affecting actions under human approval.

Privacy

PinPilot AI is intended for the owner's personal business use. Information accessed through connected services is intended to support research, analytics, and content management.
